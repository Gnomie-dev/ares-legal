Privacy Policy

Ares Discord Bot · Effective October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes data Ares may access and process when its features are used. The developer can be contacted at gnomiesgnomie@hotmail.com.

1. Information Ares processes

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, channel and message IDs needed to identify interactions and maintain features.
  • Activity information: game interactions, reactions, appreciation counts, leaderboard scores, streaks and command names or associated metadata.
  • Message content: content sent to the bot for forwarding, and messages it must read to perform configured reaction or game features.
  • Operational information: error or diagnostic information if produced by the bot's hosting or logging systems.

Not every category is necessarily stored in a database; some information may only be processed temporarily. Actual handling depends on which features are enabled and how the bot is configured.

2. Why this information is used

Ares processes information to respond to commands, run games, add reactions, calculate rankings and streaks, route forwarded messages, record configured command activity, maintain security and troubleshoot failures.

3. Where messages and logs go

Commands may be logged in a Discord channel selected for that purpose. Messages sent to Ares may be forwarded to a designated Discord channel. Authorized members with access to those channels may see those records or forwarded contents. Please do not send secrets, passwords, payment information or other sensitive information to Ares.

4. Storage and retention

Data required for ongoing leaderboards and streaks may be retained while the associated feature is in operation. Other data may be processed transiently or retained in application logs depending on deployment configuration. Messages and logs posted to Discord channels remain subject to those channels' deletion and retention practices. The operator should remove data that is no longer needed and respond to verified deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and Discord's developer rules.

5. Sharing and third-party services

Ares uses Discord to receive events and publish messages or results. Discord processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Bot hosting infrastructure may process operational data to run Ares. Ares does not require users to publish personal information to use its entertainment features, but selected scores and interaction results may be displayed in Discord channels.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop interacting with Ares and ask a server administrator to disable or remove it. To request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your Discord account, email gnomiesgnomie@hotmail.com. Please include your Discord user ID and describe the request, but do not send passwords or authentication tokens. We may need to verify the request. If information has been reposted into Discord channels, removing application database records may not itself remove those Discord messages; contact the relevant server administrator or request assistance identifying messages to delete.

7. Security

We seek to limit access to stored data and Discord logging channels to those who need it. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Report concerns via the contact email above.

8. International processing and applicable privacy law

Discord and any hosting providers may process data in jurisdictions outside your own. Applicable privacy rights, including rights under GDPR where relevant, will be respected. If required by law, additional information about the lawful basis, service providers, international transfers, and specific retention periods will be made available.

9. Changes

This policy may change as Ares features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: gnomiesgnomie@hotmail.com.
